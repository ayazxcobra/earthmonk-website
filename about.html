Our Story

Grounded in earth, warmed by fire.

The House of Earthmonk started with a simple idea: strip away the noise and let good ingredients, honest technique and a warm room do the work. Vasant Vihar's Bhayli neighbourhood gave us the space; a monk's patience gave us the name.

From wood-fired Neapolitan pizzas to slow-cooked Indian classics, every dish on our table is vegetarian, made to be shared, and built for evenings that don't need to be rushed.
